03 ROADMAP / 内容简介

文章以湖南益阳一场草根足球赛为切口，分析从“苏超”扩散至全国多省的城市业余联赛为何能同时点燃消费、地方认同和大众情绪。

比赛水平并不整齐，传球失误、射门打偏、回看耗时等问题随处可见，但球迷投入的真情远胜长期战绩不佳的国家队。

2025 年春从江苏兴起后，类似赛事已在约 20 个省份出现。

在经济增长乏力和房地产低迷的背景下，地方政府把联赛视为经济催化剂。

球场外的小吃、赠品、住宿和跨城观赛带动消费，也让千篇一律的城市文旅多了一种不可预测的戏剧性。

联赛更深的力量来自乡土认同。

长期外出工作的人借家乡球队重新建立归属感；被省会光芒遮蔽的小城市，也可以通过比赛展示本地文化、好客形象和竞争心。

相较深受腐败与假球丑闻困扰的职业联赛，草根赛事显得纯粹。

高中生上场、本地户籍、低价票和人脸识别防黄牛等规则，被用来保护业余和地方属性；它虽难直接提高国家队水平，却可能吸引更多青少年踢球。

文章最后提醒，赛事名为草根，实际多由官员自上而下组织。

若排名变成政绩指标、城市开始砸钱招揽球员，或大品牌和资本逐步接管商业合作，这种轻松真实的魅力便可能被侵蚀。

The article is about local amateur football leagues in China. It begins with a match in Yiyang, Hunan.

The players were not highly skilled, but 26,000 fans cheered loudly and cared deeply about the result. These city-versus-city competitions began in Jiangsu in 2025 and have spread to many provinces.

Local governments see the leagues as a way to help the economy. Matches bring visitors who spend money on food, hotels and local products.

In places affected by the property downturn, football has attracted people more successfully than night markets or concerts. Sport is also more exciting than similar tourist streets and attractions found in many cities.

The leagues create strong hometown pride. Many Chinese people have moved away for work, but they still feel connected to the places where they grew up.

Supporting a local team gives them a way to express this identity. Smaller cities can also show their culture to visitors.

The amateur leagues seem cleaner than professional Chinese football, which has suffered from corruption and match-fixing. Rules keep tickets cheap and require players to have local connections.

Some teams must also include high-school students. These matches may encourage more young people to play football.

However, there are risks. Officials organise most of the leagues, and cities may begin to treat results as a measure of government performance.

Large companies are also becoming sponsors. The main question is whether local football can keep its friendly grassroots spirit as money and official pressure grow.
