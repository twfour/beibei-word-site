文章由两部分组成，讨论西方奢侈品牌在中国市场增长放缓之际，中国本土高端品牌和全球中端服饰品牌如何抢走消费者。

第一部分以老铺黄金等品牌为例：当欧洲奢侈品集团在华收入下滑时，本土高端品牌依靠文化审美、产品创新和更贴近中国消费者的定位逆势增长，销量和毛利率表现亮眼。

不过投资者仍担心这股热潮只是短期风口，因为中国品牌尚未建立与欧洲百年奢侈品牌相当的全球影响力，改变国际消费者审美也需要很长时间。

文章用欧洲汽车企业在中国市场的衰落提醒奢侈品巨头：即使全球品牌优势深厚，也不能忽视本土竞争者的快速升级。

第二部分转向优衣库、Zara、Gap 等中端品牌。

奢侈品牌持续涨价，把许多消费者推向价格更合理、品质和设计不断提升的中端产品；联名设计师策略又帮助中端品牌提高调性、售价和毛利率。

奢侈品牌却陷入两难：降价会损害稀缺感和品牌价值，不降价又难以挽回流失顾客。

文章结尾指出，消费者正逐渐不再把高价标签等同于精英身份，市场规则因此被重新书写。

The article is about changes in the fashion and luxury market. Western luxury companies are earning less money in China, while some Chinese premium brands are growing quickly.

Laopu Gold is one example. It combines traditional Chinese design with modern luxury and has achieved strong sales and profit margins.

However, investors are not sure that this success will last. Chinese luxury brands still have less global influence than famous European companies.

Building an international image and changing people’s ideas about beauty can take many years. Still, the fall of European car companies in China shows that old global leaders should not ignore fast local competitors.

The second part of the article looks at middle-market clothing brands such as Uniqlo, Zara and Gap. Luxury companies have raised prices again and again, so many customers are looking for cheaper choices.

Middle-market brands now offer better quality and design than before. These companies also work with famous designers.

Such partnerships improve their image and allow them to charge more while keeping prices below luxury levels. Luxury brands cannot easily lower their prices because discounts could damage their special image.

The main message is that high prices no longer automatically show high status. Chinese brands and stronger middle-market companies are changing the rules of the fashion business.
