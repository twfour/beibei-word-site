作者认为，类似互联网泡沫的股市崩盘完全可能发生，但深度金融危机的概率较低。

过去的投资者常常看对技术方向，却看错最终赢家：修建铁路、航空和光纤网络的早期资本可能血本无归，真正获利的反而是后来利用这些基础设施建立业务的公司。

把今天与1873年铁路泡沫直接相比并不恰当。

美国AI资本支出中很多资金流向进口芯片，国内经济的直接敞口有限；现代央行也可以通过降息应对危机。

更重要的是，本轮AI投资仍主要依靠股权和科技巨头现金流，而不是高杠杆家庭债务。

如果泡沫破裂，美国家庭可能减少消费，高负债的新型云服务商可能倒闭，美国也可能经历类似2001年的轻度衰退。

除非再叠加债市恐慌或战争，危机不太可能失控。

文章最后强调，政治反弹或许能减慢AI发展，但单纯的金融崩盘大概率阻止不了技术继续前进。

AI models are becoming more powerful very quickly, and Anthropic may soon enter the stock market. However, the AI boom also looks less stable.

Nvidia is facing a scandal, American politicians may act against data centres, and technology shares have fallen. History shows that an important new technology can succeed even when many investors lose money.

The amount of money going into AI is very large. A small group of AI-related companies now makes up a big part of the American stock market, and many families own shares.

Anthropic and OpenAI bring customers into the AI supply chain. If they cannot sell enough services at high enough prices, chip companies and data centres may also suffer.

The writer thinks an AI stock-market crash is possible, but a serious financial crisis is less likely. Investors often recognise a powerful new technology but fail to choose the companies that will make money from it.

Many people lost money building airlines and fibre networks, while later companies used that infrastructure to become successful. Today is also different from the railway crash of 1873.

Much American AI spending buys imported equipment, so the direct effect on the US economy is smaller. Modern central banks can cut interest rates during a crisis.

Most AI investment is still paid for with company shares and cash, rather than dangerous levels of debt. If the AI boom ends, families may spend less and some highly indebted cloud companies may fail.

America could have a mild recession like the one in 2001. A much worse crisis would probably need another disaster at the same time.

Political action may slow AI development, but a financial crash alone will probably not stop the technology.
