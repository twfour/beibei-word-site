When 引导的时间状语从句 When the first images of the opposition leader María Corina Machado emerged from inside Venezuela after a near ten-month exile on October 25th 从句连词：When 从句主语：the first images 后置定语修饰 images：of the opposition leader María Corina Machado María Corina Machado 是 the opposition leader 的同位语 从句谓语：emerged（不及物动词：出现、流出、曝光） 地点状语：from inside Venezuela 从委内瑞拉境内 时间状语 1：after a near ten-month exile 在将近十个月的流亡之后 ten-month 带连字符，作定语修饰 exile 时间状语 2：on October 25th（具体日期） 从句直译：10 月 25 日，反对派领袖玛丽亚・科里纳・马查多身在委内瑞拉境内的首批画面流出时

“The conditions allowing for the safe return of Ms Machado to Venezuela is the direct result of the US supported reconciliation talks which has already seen hundreds of political prisoners freed and hundreds of political opposition figures return from exile,” said the American secretary of state,

1. 主句（直接引语内部的主句） The conditions [后置定语] is the direct result of the US supported reconciliation talks [定语从句]. 主语：The conditions（复数：条件） 后置定语：allowing for the safe return of Ms Machado to Venezuela allowing 现在分词作后置定语，修饰 conditions

allow for：使… 成为可能、容许 含义：使得马查多能够安全返回委内瑞拉的（各项）条件 系动词：is → 语法错误，主语是复数 conditions，规范应该是 are 表语：the direct result of … … 的直接成果 US supported：复合定语，规范写法 US-supported（加连字符，形容词：美国支持的）

2. 定语从句：which has already seen hundreds of political prisoners freed and hundreds of political opposition figures return from exile 先行词：the reconciliation talks（复数：多轮和解谈判） 关系代词 which 指代 talks（复数） 从句谓语 has seen → 主谓不一致；规范应该是 have seen 句型：see + 宾语 + 宾补（外刊高频无灵主语 see：某事见证 / 促成了某事发生）

第一个宾补：hundreds of political prisoners freed

freed 过去分词作宾补：囚犯 “被释放”（被动含义） 第二个宾补：hundreds of political opposition figures return from exile return 动词原形（不带 to 的不定式）作宾补 结构：see sb do sth 见证某人做某事

3. 引语外面的转述分句（倒装） said the American secretary of state, Marco Rubio, on October 25th. 这是直接引语前置引起的全部倒装，正常语序： The American secretary of state, Marco Rubio, said [引号内内容] on October 25th. Marco Rubio：同位语，解释说明 the American secretary of state

* 在口语、即兴演讲以及现代新闻报道中，这类错误非常常见。政治家在口头演讲或现场采访中，常常会因 为脑海中先构思了“这是一个结果（is the result）”这一整体概念，而忽略了前面复数主语（conditions / talks） 的搭配，导致说出口时出现了口误。
