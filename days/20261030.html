文章讨论美联储在通胀仍高于目标时维持利率不变的决定，既解释其谨慎理由，也警告暂缓加息可能重演误判“暂时性通胀”的错误。

支持按兵不动的第一项理由是就业。

加息会提高企业借贷成本，而美国新增岗位低于预期、数据还被下修，就业市场虽未恶化，却处于“低招聘、低裁员”的脆弱状态。

第二项理由来自市场：此前加息已推高国债收益率，金融条件本身正在收紧；市场也已高度押注利率不变，突然加息可能引发剧烈重新定价。

临近中期选举、避免政治干预质疑，也是潜在考量。

反对者担心，美联储再次把能源上涨当作短暂供给冲击。

疫情后同类判断曾令通胀失控，迫使央行后来连续大幅加息。

如今房租、服务业和 AI 数据中心投资已把价格压力扩散到更多领域。

伊朗战争和俄乌局势还可能继续推高能源价格。

货币政策传导存在滞后，如果央行等待过久，12 月可能不得不一次加息更多，给金融市场、住房和实体经济带来更大冲击。

结尾指出，2022 年激进加息曾重创股市和楼市，但美国侥幸躲过衰退。

美联储眼下是在提前压制通胀与保护就业之间做艰难选择，而经济学家并不愿再次押注软着陆。

The article is about the Federal Reserve’s decision to keep interest rates unchanged. Inflation is still above the Fed’s target, but the central bank wants to wait and see how the economy reacts to last month’s rate increase.

Interest rates affect jobs, housing, markets and many other parts of the economy. One reason for waiting is the labour market.

America added fewer jobs than expected, and earlier figures were revised down. Unemployment is still low, but companies are hiring less.

Higher rates would make borrowing more expensive and could reduce hiring further. Markets are another reason.

Government bond yields are already high, which slows the economy in a similar way to a rate increase. Investors also strongly expected the Fed to hold rates, so a surprise increase could have caused painful market changes.

The coming election may also have influenced the timing. However, waiting could be a mistake.

After the pandemic, the Fed called inflation temporary and acted too slowly. It later had to raise rates very quickly.

Today, higher energy costs are spreading into rent, services, electricity, construction and technology. Investment in AI data centres may also be overheating the economy.

The main message is that the Fed faces a difficult choice. Raising rates now could hurt jobs and markets, but waiting too long could force a much larger increase later.

There is no safe option, and another soft landing is not guaranteed.
