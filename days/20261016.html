04 EASIER ENGLISH / 简明改写

The article is about unreliable financial numbers in the artificial-intelligence industry. One report said OpenAI’s revenue was close to $50 billion, while another report gave a much higher number.

Technology shares moved sharply even though investors could not be sure which figure was correct. AI startups often use ARR, or annual recurring revenue.

This number takes recent income and imagines that it will continue for a full year. It is useful for fast-growing businesses, but it is not the same as normal audited revenue.

Companies may also count the same type of sales in different ways. OpenAI and Anthropic are private companies, so they do not have to publish complete financial reports.

Investors depend on leaked information and selected numbers. Companies can also use adjusted profit figures that remove costs they do not want investors to focus on.

Large public technology companies are more transparent, but their AI results are still difficult to measure. They spend huge amounts on data centres and chips.

This quickly helps chip and construction companies, while the cost appears more slowly in the technology companies’ own accounts. The real return from AI is still uncertain.

The main message is that AI may be valuable, but the numbers used to price it are often unclear or impossible to compare. Investors, journalists and governments need stronger accounting, auditing and economic statistics.
