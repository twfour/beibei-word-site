The article is about competition between China, Europe and America in electric vehicles and battery recycling. The European Union plans to limit imports of Chinese hybrid cars.

At the same time, Western countries want to keep valuable battery waste instead of sending it to China. Old batteries can be broken into a powder called black mass.

Useful metals such as lithium, cobalt and nickel can be recovered from it and used again. China wants more recycled materials because this can reduce its need for newly mined minerals and foreign supplies.

China’s recycling industry has changed greatly. In the past, the country imported large amounts of foreign waste, and small informal businesses often caused pollution.

The government later banned most solid-waste imports and supported cleaner recycling companies and urban mines. China has an important advantage because it makes many batteries, solar panels and wind turbines itself.

Its factories, recyclers and metal processors are close together. However, many used batteries still go to informal dealers or are simply thrown away.

The government is now improving collection, digital tracking and AI sorting. The main message is that Europe and America can keep more of their own waste, but building a complete recycling industry is expensive and difficult.

China is likely to remain the leading battery recycler for some time.

1. 主句： Much of China’s recycling capacity is unused. 主语：Much of China’s recycling capacity much of + 不可数名词：…… 中的大部分；capacity 不可数 谓语：is unused（系表结构，被动含义，unused 形容词作表语：未被利用的、闲置的）

2. because 引导原因状语从句（解释主句） because too many batteries end up with informal scrap dealers (who waste much of the good stuff inside them) or are just dumped.

原因状语从句内部，主语是 too many batteries，后面有两个并列谓语，由 or 连接： 谓语①：end up with informal scrap dealers（主动） end up with：最终落入…… 手中 谓语②：are just dumped（一般现在时被动语态） 还原：too many batteries are just dumped（电池被丢弃）

3. 括号内：who 引导限制性定语从句，修饰先行词 informal scrap dealers (who waste much of the good stuff inside them) who：关系代词，指代 informal scrap dealers，在从句中作主语 从句谓语：waste 宾语：much of the good stuff inside them：介词短语后置定语，修饰 good stuff；them 指代 batteries
