1. 主语内部 the plight of the late María del Carmen Abascal plight：n. 困境、悲惨境遇 late：adj. 已故的 破折号插入并列成分：and the wider issue it embodied it embodied 是定语从句，先行词 the wider issue；关系代词 that 省略（that 在从句作宾语） it = the plight（这件悲剧） embody：v. 体现、代表 the wider issue (that) it embodied = 这件事所折射出的更宏大的社会问题 破折号在这里是插入语，起到补充、拓宽主题的作用：不只是一个老太太的个人悲剧，而是一个社会矛盾的 缩影。

2. 第一个谓语：led to led to street demonstrations and the creation of a protest camp in the middle of the Spanish capital lead to + 名词性宾语（to 是介词，后面不能接动词原形） 两个宾语并列： ① street demonstrations ② the creation of a protest camp

in the middle of the Spanish capital 介词短语后置，修饰 camp

3. 第二个谓语：spurred and spurred the socialist-led government to propose emergency measures to facilitate and protect access to housing spur sb. to do sth. 固定搭配：促使 / 激励某人做某事 the socialist-led government：过去分词 socialist-led 作前置定语，由社会党领衔的政府 to propose emergency measures：不定式作宾语补足语 to facilitate and protect access to housing：不定式后置定语，修饰 measures facilitate and protect 并列两个动词原形，共用 to access to housing：获得住房的渠道、安居权
