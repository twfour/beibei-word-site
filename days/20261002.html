2. where 定语从句（非限制性定语从句，修饰 Tabuk） where = in which（在该地），从句主干： passengers disembarked … and waited for a replacement aircraft 从句内部是并列谓语： a. passengers disembarked（乘客下飞机） b. and waited for a replacement aircraft（乘客等候备用飞机） 并列主语同为 passengers，第二个谓语 waited 承前省略主语 passengers。

3. 插入独立主格：, some of them in bloodstained clothing, 结构：名词短语 + 介词短语，独立主格，作伴随状语，修饰 passengers。 还原完整：some of them were in bloodstained clothing 含义：其中部分人身着染血的衣物。

没有连词，只用来补充描述乘客状态，不构成独立句子

4. 后置定语：sent by FlyDubai 过去分词短语，修饰前面 a replacement aircraft = a replacement aircraft that was sent by FlyDubai 迪拜航空派出的备用飞机 不定式：to take them on to Tel Aviv 目的状语，修饰 sent（或者说修饰 replacement aircraft）： （这架备用飞机）将把他们继续送往特拉维夫。 take sb on to + 地点：继续运送某人前往某地（中途经停后继续前往最终目的地）

SENTENCE 2
Israeli officials said on Wednesday that much about the traumatic flight remained unclear, including why a pilot would have tried to crash the plane, and whether it was specifically an attack on Israelis, as many people in Israel — including some in the government — quickly concluded.

1. 句子主干 Israeli officials said [宾语从句] 主语：Israeli officials 谓语：said 状语：on Wednesday（时间状语） 后面一整段是 that 引导的宾语从句，作 said 的宾语。 以色列官员周三表示，这起惊魂航班尚有诸多疑点。

2. that 宾语从句内部 that much about the traumatic flight remained unclear, … 从句主语：much（代词，很多事情、诸多情况，不可数） 后置定语：about the traumatic flight 修饰 much 系动词：remained（仍然处于） 表语：unclear 核心：much … remained unclear 诸多情况仍不明朗 后面的 including … 介词短语，用来列举 “不明朗的两件事”： a. why a pilot would have tried to crash the plane 宾语从句 1（why 引导，作 including 的宾语） b. whether it was specifically an attack on Israelis

宾语从句 2（whether 引导，作 including 的宾语） including 在这里是介词，后面接两个并列的名词性从句：why 从句 + whether 从句。

3. 末尾状语从句：as many people in Israel — including some in the government — quickly concluded as 引导方式状语从句，as = 正如、就像 主语：many people in Israel 插入语：— including some in the government — 破折号插入，补充说明 people 谓语：concluded（断定、得出结论）
