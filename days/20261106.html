投资者的平静首先来自不确定性消退。

民主党明确胜出，避免了长期争议选举结果甚至宪政危机的最坏情形；特朗普也表示接受结果，带来“松了一口气”效应。

市场同样乐见分裂政府带来的政策僵局。

民主党控制国会后，总统提高关税、发动战争和大幅削减公共部门将更受制约；民主党也较少用阻挠提高债务上限来威胁政府违约。

不过，大盘的平静掩盖了个股和行业的暗流。

马斯克曾花近5亿美元帮助共和党竞选，特斯拉和SpaceX可能面临民主党对政府合同的审查，股价分别下跌3%和4%。

私人监狱运营商因移民执法预算可能收紧而下跌，洛克希德·马丁、诺斯罗普·格鲁曼、RTX等军工企业也受到国防支出削减预期冲击。

受打击更大的是AI数据中心产业链。

数据中心的耗电、用水、噪音和环境问题引发反弹，得州候任民主党州长承诺暂停新建项目。

CoreWeave下跌10%，美光下跌7%，英伟达下跌3%，多家电力设备公司跌幅约一成，因为民主党可能对能源项目许可提出更苛刻条件。

反过来，医疗保健和清洁能源可能受益于特朗普预算削减被撤销。

亚马逊、微软、Alphabet和Meta等超大规模云服务商也可能减少数据中心支出，同时面临更少的CoreWeave类竞争，从而保住利润率。

文章的核心判断是：选举结果对整体市场影响有限，但国会监管、预算和许可权的变化，正在重新分配不同企业和行业的政治风险。

The article explains how financial markets reacted to the Democrats' big victory in the 2026 US midterm elections. The political result was dramatic, but the broad market response was calm.

The S&P 500 rose by 1.6%, government-bond yields moved down slightly and the dollar became a little weaker. Investors were calm because they like certainty.

A clear result prevented a long argument about the election and reduced the risk of a constitutional crisis. Donald Trump also said that he would accept the result.

Markets also often like divided government because it makes sudden and extreme policy changes more difficult. However, some companies lost money.

Elon Musk had spent almost $500 million helping Republicans. A Democratic Congress may now examine government contracts with Tesla and SpaceX more carefully.

Private-prison companies and defence firms may also receive less government money. Companies connected to AI data centres were another group of losers.

Local people are worried about electricity use, water, noise and pollution. CoreWeave, Micron and Nvidia all fell, and several electrical-equipment companies also lost value.

Democrats may make new data-centre and energy projects harder to approve. Other businesses gained.

Health-care and clean-energy companies may benefit if earlier budget cuts are reversed. Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet and Meta may spend less on new data centres and face less competition from independent operators.

The main message is that the whole market did not panic, but the election changed which industries investors expect to win or lose.
