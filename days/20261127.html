03 ROADMAP / 内容简介

文章先以深圳峰会为切口：会议没有取得重要成果，却显示美国、中国和俄罗斯仍掌握大国谈判桌。

作者认为，这恰好印证了卡尼的判断——中等强国必须合作，否则只能被大国安排。

加拿大已通过加入欧洲防务采购、拓展自由贸易协定和建设替代性卫星通信，把这一构想付诸实践；欧洲在美国撤资后继续援助乌克兰，多国联合战机项目也显示合作已有成果。

随后，文章分析中等强国摆脱依赖的三重障碍。

技术上，它们依赖美国的数字平台，并在人工智能领域同时落后于中美；能源上，欧洲减少对美国化石燃料的依赖，可能转而依赖中国主导的新能源产业链；外交上，各国的安全、地区和意识形态利益不同，很难选出统一代表。

文章最终指出，中等强国虽有合作必要，也取得了一些进展，但要形成一个能够坐上大国谈判桌的共同声音，仍面临深层结构性困难。

This supported Canadian prime minister Mark Carney's idea that middle powers should work together. Canada has tried to turn this idea into action.

It has joined a European defence-buying plan and made new trade agreements. It also wants satellite systems that do not depend on the United States.

European countries have supported Ukraine after America reduced its funding, and several middle powers are developing a new fighter plane together. However, co-operation has limits.

Europe, Canada and Australia depend heavily on American technology. In artificial intelligence, American and Chinese companies have far more money and computing power than their competitors.

Middle powers may therefore become even more dependent on the two AI superpowers. Energy creates another problem.

Europe can reduce its dependence on American fossil fuels by using more renewable energy. But China leads the world in rare-earth minerals, batteries, solar panels and wind turbines.

Europe may simply exchange one dependence for another. Diplomacy is also difficult because middle powers do not always agree.

Europe has many possible representatives, while countries in Asia, Latin America, Africa and the Middle East have different interests. The article concludes that middle powers need to act together, but building one clear and powerful voice will be very hard.
