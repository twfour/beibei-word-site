The writer says he got the job mainly because he was loyal to Donald Trump. After taking office, Hegseth began removing officers and civilian officials from the Pentagon.

The article says these people were not removed because they were bad at their jobs. They were removed because Hegseth thought they were not loyal enough to him.

One important official, Army Secretary Dan Driscoll, resigned after disagreements with him. The writer argues that the Defense Department is a huge and complicated organization.

A good defense secretary must understand the defense industry, alliances, Congress and military planning. Hegseth, in the writer’s view, does not understand these things well enough.

The article also criticizes his support for Trump’s attack on Iran. Other senior officials warned that the war could cause serious problems, but Hegseth supported the idea of a quick victory.

Later, the U.S. military had trouble keeping up with the conflict. The main message is that loyalty is not enough for such an important job.

If a leader cares more about personal loyalty than professional advice, the army and the country may pay a serious price.

SENTENCE STUDY 01 / 02
SENTENCE 1
A television host with little background in national-security or defense affairs, who topped out as a major in the National Guard, he was nominated based on the same qualification shared by most of Donald Trump’s other appointees: loyalty.

1. 同位语：A television host … affairs

A television host 核心名词，和主句主语 he 指同一个人，身份同位语。 with little background in …：介词短语作后置定语，修饰 host little：否定含义，几乎没有（不是 “一点”） background in sth：某领域的阅历、从业背景

2. who topped out as a major in the National Guard 非限制性定语从句，关系代词 who = a television host（即 Hegseth） top out as：固定习语，最高做到；职业生涯到此封顶，再也升不上去。 who topped out as a major：他军衔最高止步于少校，暗含贬义：仕途天花板很低。 major：（美军）少校 National Guard：国民警卫队

3. 主句部分：he was nominated 被动语态：他被提名（出任国防部长） based on the same qualification，过去分词短语作方式状语，修饰 was nominated：提名的依据是这一项资历。 shared by most of Donald Trump’s other appointees 过去分词作后置定语，修饰 qualification appointee：被任命者、政府任命官员 qualification：此处不取 “学历资质”，语境义：入选资本、得以上位的条件。 : loyalty 冒号，同位语，解释说明 qualification：这项资本就是忠诚。

SENTENCE STUDY 02 / 02
SENTENCE 2
Even then–Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard, another of Trump’s unqualified-but- sycophantic appointees, mustered the courage to give the president bad news, telling Trump that killing the reigning ayatollah and striking Iran would “likely usher in a more hard-line regime open to acquiring nuclear weapons”; that the Iranians “would rush to close the Strait of Hormuz, destabilizing the global energy market”; and that Iran “would strike U.S. forces and partners in the Middle East,” all of which happened almost immediately after Trump gave the order to go to war.

1. 主语部分 Even then–Director of National Intelligence Tulsi Gabbard then-Director of National Intelligence：时任国家情报总监。复合形容词 then- = 当时任职的；修饰人名 Tulsi Gabbard。 Even：副词，加强语气：即便是、就连。潜台词：连这样一个阿谀奉承的任命官员，都敢说逆耳真话，反衬 赫格塞思的懦弱。 , another of Trump’s unqualified-but-sycophantic appointees, 插入语，同位语，补充说明 Tulsi Gabbard。 unqualified-but-sycophantic：合成形容词，能力不足但善于逢迎谄媚的。 syncophantic：谄媚的、奉承的。

2. 谓语动词 mustered the courage：固定搭配，鼓起勇气

to give the president bad news：不定式作后置定语修饰 courage：鼓起勇气去给总统带去坏消息（进逆耳忠 言）。

3. telling Trump that … ; that … ; and that … 现在分词短语 telling 作伴随状语，逻辑主语是 Tulsi Gabbard，说明 “鼓起勇气” 做的具体行为：告诉总统三 件事。 tell sb that A ; that B ; and that C

宾语从句① that killing the reigning ayatollah and striking Iran would “likely usher in a more hard-line regime open to acquiring nuclear weapons” killing … and striking Iran：两个动名词并列，作从句主语：诛杀伊朗最高领袖、打击伊朗。 would usher in：将会催生、带来；usher in：开启，引发。 hard-line regime：强硬派政权。 open to acquiring nuclear weapons：形容词短语作后置定语修饰 regime：倾向于谋求核武器的。open to：愿 意、倾向于做某事。

宾语从句② that the Iranians “would rush to close the Strait of Hormuz, destabilizing the global energy market” would rush to close：会迅速封锁。 destabilizing the global energy market：现在分词作结果状语：（此举）扰乱全球能源市场。

宾语从句③ and that Iran “would strike U.S. forces and partners in the Middle East” 简单宾语从句：伊朗将会袭击中东美军以及盟友。

4. 句尾：all of which happened almost immediately after Trump gave the order to go to war. 非限制性定语从句 which 指代前面引号里全部三条警告内容（三件事），所以用 all of which。 all of which = all of those three outcomes（所有这些情况） 内部嵌套时间状语从句：after Trump gave the order to go to war gave the order to do：下达…… 的命令；to go to war 不定式后置修饰 order。
