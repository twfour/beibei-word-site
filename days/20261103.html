文章指出，中美贸易战虽然暂时淡出头条、加征关税也因休战而降温，但供应链和技术领域正在酝酿一场规模更大的经济战争。

双方没有建立可约束新冲突的互信机制，反而都在为下一轮对抗争取优势，并把经济、科技和军事领先定义为国家安全核心。

新的军备竞赛不只制造武器，更在于增强自身供应链韧性，同时准备限制甚至切断对手的关键投入。

美国从拜登时期的“小院高墙”出发，限制少数军民两用先进技术，后来出口、投资和技术转让管制不断扩张，已影响中国整体技术进步；特朗普广泛使用关税又进一步扩大冲突。

中国则利用关键矿产和稀土磁体的主导地位反制芯片与设备限制，并把部分出口管制用于其他外交争端。

国际能源署估计，若美欧失去中国稀土磁体供应，每年经济损失可能约达 1.5 万亿美元。

美国正投资本土稀土开采加工，并继续封锁前沿芯片和制造设备；中国也限制 AI 芯片、太阳能等高科技出口，并警告试图降低对华依赖的国家。

和平需要双方共同界定真正的国家安全风险，避免把普通商业摩擦军事化。

文章用二战前资源封锁与日本袭击珍珠港的历史警示，说明供应链“卡脖子”可能外溢成真实战争。

贸易曾被视为以相互依存促进和平的工具，如今这种依存却被当作弱点和战场。

04 EASIER ENGLISH / 简明改写

The article is about the hidden economic war between China and the United States. The public trade war looks quieter because the two countries have extended a temporary truce.

However, both sides are preparing for a much larger conflict over technology and supply chains. The new arms race is not only about weapons.

Each country wants stronger supply chains for itself and the power to limit important supplies to its rival. The United States first described its policy as a small yard of sensitive technology protected by a high fence.

Its controls have since become much wider. China has responded by using its strong position in critical minerals and rare-earth magnets.

These materials are needed for civilian technology and military equipment. If China blocked American and European access to rare-earth magnets, the economic cost could reach about $1.5 trillion a year.

America is spending money on its own mines and processing plants while limiting China’s access to advanced chips and chip-making machines. China is also restricting exports of high technology and warning countries not to reduce their dependence on Chinese minerals.

The writer hopes both sides can agree on which risks are truly about national security. History shows that blocking access to raw materials can help cause real wars.

The main message is that trade once created peaceful interdependence, but now both countries increasingly see interdependence as a dangerous weakness.
